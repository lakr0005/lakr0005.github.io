Life in the Mountains

Mountains are one of the most beautiful natural landscapes on Earth. They offer fresh air, amazing views, and peaceful environments. Many people travel to mountains to relax and enjoy nature.

Hiking, camping, and exploring wildlife are popular activities. Living close to nature can reduce stress and improve mental health.

Facts About Mountains

  • Mount Everest is the highest mountain in the world.
  • Mountains cover about 22% of Earth's land surface.
  • They are home to unique plants and animals.
